### Week 6 Objectives:

- Study AWS storage and data management solutions, including Amazon FSx, Data Lake and Amazon DynamoDB.
- Explore AWS cost optimization methods, selecting appropriate EC2 configurations and tracking usage costs.
- Get familiar with AWS Glue, Amazon Athena and the process of migrating monolithic applications.
- Study microservices architecture, automated application deployment and data restructuring workflows.

### Week 6 Objectives:

- Study AWS storage and data management solutions, including Amazon FSx, Data Lake and Amazon DynamoDB.
- Explore AWS cost optimization methods, selecting appropriate EC2 configurations and tracking usage costs.
- Get familiar with AWS Glue, Amazon Athena and the process of migrating monolithic applications.
- Study microservices architecture, automated application deployment and data restructuring workflows.

### Week 6 Achievements:

- Gained a better understanding of Amazon FSx, Data Lakes, and Amazon DynamoDB, along with data organization and management on AWS.
- Learned about AWS cost optimization options, including Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, Reserved DB Instances, and EC2 right-sizing.
- Became familiar with AWS Glue and Amazon Athena for cost analysis and explored key considerations in monolithic application migration.
- Improved understanding of microservices and automated application releases, including the benefits of modular design and deployment automation.
- Understood the role of data and workflow refactoring in improving system maintainability, reducing complexity, and supporting scalability.

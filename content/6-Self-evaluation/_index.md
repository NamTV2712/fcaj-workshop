During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** from **14 September 2026** to **30 November 2026**, I worked as an **AI Engineer intern** in the **Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey** program, contributing to **TINA – an AI meeting assistant on AWS**. I helped design and implement the containerized AWS architecture (ECS/Fargate, VPC, ALB, RDS, ElastiCache, S3, Cognito, Bedrock and SES) and focused on the AI parts: the "Ask TINA" agent on Amazon Bedrock, live Vietnamese transcription with English/bilingual translation, wake-word and TTS spoken replies, and post-meeting notes, summaries and follow-up emails. Through the project I improved my skills in generative AI integration, prompt and context design, Python/TypeScript services, Docker, CI/CD, cost optimization and cross-functional communication.

In terms of work ethic, I consistently strove to complete tasks well, complied with workplace regulations, and actively exchanged ideas with teammates to improve work efficiency.
